# 💪 **Chest & Leg Workout Plan**  
*Balanced strength training for muscle growth and endurance*  

---

## 🔥 **Chest Workout**  

### 🧘 **Warm-Up**  
- 5-10 minutes of dynamic stretches (arm circles, shoulder rotations)  
- 1-2 light sets of push-ups  

---

### 1️⃣ **Bodyweight Exercises**  
| Exercise               | Reps 🔢 | Sets 🏋️ | Rest ⏱️  | Focus 🎯         |  
|------------------------|---------|---------|---------|------------------|  
| 📉 Flat Push-ups       | 50      | 4       | 30-45s  | Mid-Chest        |  
| 📈 Incline Push-ups    | 50      | 4       | 30-45s  | Lower Chest      |  
| 📊 Decline Push-ups    | 50      | 4       | 30-45s  | Upper Chest      |

## 🦵 **Leg Workout**

- 5-10 minutes of leg swings, bodyweight squats, or jumping jacks  

---

### 1️⃣ **Bodyweight Exercises**  
| Exercise               | Reps 🔢 | Sets 🏋️ | Rest ⏱️  | Focus 🎯       |  
|------------------------|---------|---------|---------|----------------|  
| Bodyweight Squats      | 50      | 4       | 30-45s  | Quads/Glutes    |  
| Jump Squats            | 30      | 4       | 30-45s  | Explosive Power |  
| Lunges (Each Leg)      | 20      | 4       | 30-45s  | Glutes/Hamstrings |
